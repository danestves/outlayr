# Translation glossary

Product terms for Italian, Dutch, Japanese and Korean. One rendering per term, everywhere: app, web app, site, emails and store listings. German, Spanish, French and Portuguese keep the words already in their dictionaries.

## Voice per language

| Language | Address | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| it | `tu` | Sentence case. Decimal comma. Quotes “ ”. |
| nl | `je` / `jij`, never `u` | Sentence case. Decimal comma. Keep `app`, `widget`, `tag` in English. |
| ja | です・ます | Never `あなた`: drop the subject. Full-width `。、「」`, half-width digits, no space between Japanese and Latin or digits. Buttons are short nouns (`保存`, `削除`). No `！`. |
| ko | 해요체 | Never `당신`: drop the subject. Follow 띄어쓰기. Buttons are short nouns (`저장`, `삭제`). No `!`. |

Everywhere: plain and calm, no exclamation marks, no em dashes, `…` as one character, `{placeholders}` verbatim. `Outlayr`, `Pro`, `Outlayr Pro`, `Apple Pay`, `Apple Wallet`, `Google Wallet`, `Apple Watch`, `iPhone`, `Android`, `Cashea` and `BCV` are never translated.
