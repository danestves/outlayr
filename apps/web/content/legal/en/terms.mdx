### How captures are counted

- A capture is one voice clip, one receipt photo, or one installment plan screenshot. Voice entries, receipt scans, and installment plan imports share one allowance.
- A capture counts once our server accepts it, before the AI reads it. A clip with no transaction in it, a photo with no receipt or plan in it, and a spoken correction each count as one.

- Separate short-term limits apply to how quickly you can send captures, merchant lookups, and imports. When you reach one, voice or scanning pauses briefly and the app shows when the next one is available.

## AI features

Some features use artificial intelligence, run by the third-party AI providers named in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy). They need an account and an internet connection.

- **Voice entry.** You describe a transaction out loud. It is transcribed and turned into a draft entry, which you can correct by speaking again.
- **Receipt scan.** You take a photo of a receipt. The total, store, date, and line items are read into a draft entry.
- **Installment plan import.** You choose a screenshot of an order’s payment plan. Each installment’s amount, due date, late fee, and whether it is paid are read into a draft plan.
- **Merchant suggestions.** From a merchant’s name, Outlayr suggests a category, tags, and the business’s website, which is used to show its logo. You can turn this off in Settings, “Smart fill”.
- **AI matching on import (Pro).** When you import a CSV file, merchant names your phone could not match are matched to your categories. Amounts, dates, and the file itself are not sent.
- **Web merchant lookup (Pro).** For a merchant nobody has seen before, the AI may search the web to learn its category. The answer helps later entries, not the one you are entering. You can turn this off in Settings, “Smart fill”.

### AI output can be wrong

AI can mishear, misread, or guess. It can get an amount, currency, date, merchant, category, or line item wrong, or miss one entirely.

- Voice entries, receipt scans, and installment plan imports produce a draft. Nothing is saved until you tap Save, and it is up to you to check the draft first.
- Some suggestions are applied without a separate review, such as a logo looked up again in the background after you save an entry. Check your entries and correct anything that is wrong.
- If you save a wrong amount, the entry, your balances, and your budgets stay wrong until you fix it. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for losses that come from relying on AI output you did not check.

AI output is a suggestion, not a statement of fact by us. Usage limits are described in [Free and Pro](#free-and-pro).

## Backups and data loss

Outlayr is local-first. Your ledger is stored on your phone. Unless you use sync, it exists nowhere else, and we cannot recover it for you if the phone is lost, reset, or damaged, or if you delete the app.

Ways to protect it, in Settings under Export data:

- **Export a full archive.** It holds every entry, category, and budget, and it is the file you restore from. Keep a recent one somewhere other than your phone.
- **Export CSV.** A readable copy of your transactions, not a backup. Importing it re-creates only each transaction’s date, name, amount, currency, and type.

**Sync** (Pro) keeps a copy in your account, but it is not a backup either: it carries your deletions and edits to your other devices too.

Some actions cannot be undone:

- Restoring from an archive replaces everything on your phone with the archive’s contents.
- Erasing data in Settings deletes those records permanently.
- If you sign in on a phone that holds entries while your account holds others, the app asks what to keep. Two of the three choices permanently delete the entries on one side.

Entries you delete one at a time go to Recently deleted, where you can restore them.

We take reasonable care with data on our servers, but no system is free of failures. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for lost data that a recent export would have preserved.
