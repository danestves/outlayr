# Outlayr pricing

Outlayr is a mobile expense tracker on Android from Google Play, and coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. It is free to start, with no ads on any plan. Outlayr Pro raises the limits and adds sync.

| Plan | US price | Billing |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | |
| Outlayr Pro monthly | $7.99 | Every month |
| Outlayr Pro yearly | $49.99 | Every year, with 7 days free |
| Outlayr Pro once | $129.99 | One payment, no renewal |

Prices vary by country. Google Play and the App Store show your price before you buy.

## Limits

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Voice, receipt, or payment plan captures | 20 every 30 days | 500 every 30 days |
| Accounts | 2 | Unlimited |
| Installment apps | 2 | Unlimited |
| People you lend to or borrow from | 3 | Unlimited |
| Payoff plans | 2 | Unlimited |
| Category budgets | 2 | Unlimited |
| Account currencies | 1 | Unlimited |

## Included in every plan

Logging by sentence, voice, or receipt, budgets, savings goals, insights, subscriptions, debts, installments, CSV import and export, and iPhone widgets.

## Only in Pro

- Sync across devices
- Receipt photo backup
- AI matching of category names on CSV import
- Web merchant lookup
- 5 app icons on iPhone
- The ledger MCP server for AI assistants, which reads what your devices have synced
