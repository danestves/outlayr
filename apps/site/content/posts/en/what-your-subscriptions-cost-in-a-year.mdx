Nobody signs up for a lot of subscriptions. They sign up for one at a time, each small enough not to think about.

Then the list grows. Someone in London pays for two streaming plans in GBP. Someone in Manila pays for cloud storage billed in USD. A gym in Guadalajara takes MXN on the 3rd. A note-taking app in Madrid renews once a year in EUR. A music plan in Mumbai takes INR every month.

Each charge looks fine on its own. Added up, over a year, in one currency, most people have never seen the total.

## A subscription is an entry that repeats

In Outlayr, a subscription is an entry with a schedule. Log the charge the way you log anything else, then set it to repeat: daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, or every few weeks or months.

Each subscription keeps its own currency. The cloud storage stays $2.99 even if the account it comes out of is in PHP, and the account moves by what that was in pesos at the rate on the day of the charge.

Outlayr logs each charge on its date, so the Log stays true without you typing the same thing every month.

## One total, in one currency

Insights adds every subscription into one monthly figure in your main currency, then multiplies it out: "5 subscriptions cost you £64 a month. That's £768 a year. Next charge: Gym on Dec 3."

Weekly charges count as 52 a year, yearly ones as a twelfth a month, so a gym billed every week and an app billed once a year land on the same scale.

That yearly line is usually the one that makes people open the list. "See subscriptions" lists them one by one, so you can see which ones carry the total.

## A reminder before it renews

Each subscription can remind you before it charges: on the day, or 1, 2 or 7 days before. The reminder is a notification your phone schedules on its own, so it works without sync and without being signed in.

Seven days is the one worth using for yearly renewals. A yearly charge is the one you forget, and the one that hurts most when it lands.

The [subscription reminders help page](/help/how-do-subscription-reminders-work) has the details.

## What it doesn't do

Outlayr doesn't find subscriptions for you. You add each one once, by typing, speaking, or scanning the receipt.

It doesn't cancel them either. Cancelling happens where you subscribed: the App Store, Google Play, or the company's own site. These guides walk through it:

- [How to cancel a subscription](/guides/how-to-cancel-a-subscription)
- [Cancel an App Store subscription](/guides/cancel-app-store-subscription)
- [Cancel a Google Play subscription](/guides/cancel-google-play-subscription)

## Free, with no cap

Subscriptions have no limit on the free plan. Add all of them, in any currency your entries use. The 2-currency limit on the free plan is about accounts, not about what a subscription bills in.

If you want the number before you install anything, the [subscription cost calculator](/tools/subscription-cost-calculator) adds up a list in the browser. The [subscription tracker guide](/guides/subscription-tracker) covers the rest of the setup.
