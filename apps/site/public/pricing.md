# Outlayr pricing

Outlayr is the expense tracker that never links your bank: log by sentence, voice, receipt or Apple Pay, in any of 165 currencies. It is on the App Store for iPhone, on Google Play for Android, and on the web at https://app.tryoutlayr.com with Pro. It is free to start, with no ads on any plan. Outlayr Pro raises the limits and adds sync. Cancel anytime from your app store, or buy it once.

| Plan | US price | Billing |

Prices vary by country. Google Play and the App Store show your price before you buy.

## Limits

A capture is one voice clip, one receipt photo, or one installment plan import of up to 5 screenshots, and needs an account or Pro. Typing a sentence never uses one.

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|

| Auto-logged entries | 30 a month | Unlimited |
| Accounts | 3 | Unlimited |
| Installment apps | 2 | Unlimited |

## Included in every plan

Logging by sentence, voice, or receipt, budgets, savings goals, insights, subscriptions, debts, installments, CSV import and export, and widgets.
